About

Bridging fundamental physics and practical hardware

I recently completed my B.Sc. in Electrical and Electronic Engineering at Chittagong University of Engineering and Technology (CUET), working at the intersection of nanophotonics, VLSI, and applied machine learning. My interests center on scalable, chip-integrated photonic technologies for medical diagnostics and quantum information processing.

My work is both computational and experimental: day to day I build device simulations in COMSOL Multiphysics and Lumerical FDTD, design and verify custom CMOS circuits in Cadence Virtuoso, and analyze biomedical signals with explainable machine learning.

Before focusing on metasurface design, I published peer-reviewed work in biomedical signal processing — including large-scale EEG memory decoding and spectrogram-driven emotion recognition. Outside research, I follow and play football, and enjoy a good technical argument.

Research Interests

Where I focus

Four threads, one aim: moving capability onto the chip — in light, in silicon, and in the algorithms that read physical signals.

01

Nanophotonics & Metasurfaces

Designing, simulating, and characterizing nanophotonic devices — reconfigurable metasurfaces, optical tweezers, and integrated waveguides — using phase-change materials for dynamic, on-chip control of light.

02

VLSI & IC Design

Full-custom digital and analog hardware: transistor-level CMOS design, standard-cell layout, and the complete DRC / LVS / parasitic-extraction flow toward manufacturable chips.

03

Quantum Photonics & Technologies

Chip-integrated platforms for quantum information — exploring neutral-atom approaches, integrated photonics, and the device physics that makes quantum technologies scalable.

04

Biomedical Optics & ML

Optical and computational techniques for diagnostics — photonic biosensing, light–tissue interaction, and explainable machine learning applied to EEG and medical imaging.

Reconfigurable On-Chip Optical Tweezers and Beam Splitters Using Metasurfaces and Phase-Change Materials

Currently working on

June 2025 – Ongoing

  • Targeting chip-scale tunability that switches a single device between optical-tweezer and beam-splitter behaviour.
  • Investigating phase-change materials — GST, GSST, VO₂, In₃SbTe₂, Sb₂S₃, Sb₂Se₃ — for low-loss reconfiguration.

Spectrogram-Driven Emotion Detection from Electroencephalogram

Published

July 2024 – Dec. 2024

  • Converted EEG to time–frequency spectrograms with the Short-Time Fourier Transform (STFT) for CNN-based recognition.
  • Reached 99.80% accuracy classifying positive / negative / neutral states from processed spectrograms.
  • Showed raw spectrograms dropped accuracy to 66%, underscoring the role of preprocessing.
Supervisor: Md. Fazlul Karim Khondakar, Assistant Professor, Dept. of Biomedical Engineering, CUET.
